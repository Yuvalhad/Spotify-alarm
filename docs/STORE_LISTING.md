## Full description (English)

**Wake up to music you actually love.**

WakeTune connects to your Spotify and turns your mornings around: at alarm
time it plays a song from your Liked Songs, Top Tracks, playlists or favorite
artists — and it won't go quiet until you prove you're really awake.

**Pick your wake-up challenge:**
🎤 **Sing along** — keep singing until the meter fills
⌨️ **Type it out** — type the song title or artist correctly
💃 **Dance** — shake and move to hit your movement goal
🎲 **Surprise me** — a random challenge every morning

**Made for real mornings:**
• Choose difficulty — easy, medium or hard
• Gradual volume — start gentle, end impossible to ignore
• Repeat schedules for any days of the week
• Built-in fallback alarm sound — the alarm ALWAYS rings, even offline
• A hidden emergency stop, because safety comes first

**Privacy first:** no account, no server, no ads, no tracking. Your singing
is analyzed on your phone in real time and never recorded. Your Spotify
tokens stay in your device's secure storage.

**Good to know:**
• Starting full songs at alarm time requires **Spotify Premium** (a Spotify
  rule). Free accounts get the built-in alarm sound.
• On iPhone, if the app is fully closed, the alarm rings as a notification
  and your song starts when you open it — iOS limits what closed apps can do.

Requires a Spotify account. WakeTune is not affiliated with or endorsed by
Spotify.
