# WakeTune — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-07-07_

WakeTune is a smart alarm clock that wakes you with music from your Spotify
account and asks you to complete a short wake-up challenge.

**The short version: WakeTune has no server. Everything stays on your device.**

## Data we collect

**None.** WakeTune does not operate any backend service, does not create
accounts, and does not transmit your data to us or to any third party other
than Spotify (see below).

## Data stored on your device only

- **Alarms and app settings** — stored locally on your device.
- **Spotify access tokens** — stored in your device's secure storage (iOS
  Keychain / Android Keystore). Used exclusively to call Spotify's official
  API on your behalf.

## Spotify

When you connect Spotify, you authenticate directly with Spotify using their
official OAuth flow. WakeTune never sees your Spotify password. WakeTune
requests only the permissions it needs: reading your saved tracks, top
tracks, playlists and profile (to check Premium status), and controlling
playback for the alarm. Spotify's own privacy policy applies to your Spotify
account: https://www.spotify.com/legal/privacy-policy/

You can revoke WakeTune's access at any time at
https://www.spotify.com/account/apps/

## Microphone (sing-along challenge)

If you enable the optional sing-along challenge, WakeTune analyzes the
microphone's loudness level **in real time, in memory, on your device** while
the challenge is active. **No audio is recorded, saved, or uploaded —
anywhere, ever.** The microphone is used only during an active challenge and
released immediately after.

## Motion sensors (dance challenge)

Accelerometer readings are processed on-device to count your movements during
the dance challenge. They are not stored or transmitted.

## Analytics, ads, tracking

WakeTune contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising, and no trackers.

## Children

WakeTune is not directed at children under 13 and requires a Spotify account,
which has its own age requirements.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same URL
with a new "Last updated" date.
